Support

Something not working, a dose that looks wrong, or a brand or recipe you’d like to see? Open an issue on GitHub. For a problem, include your phone model and what you were doing when it happened.

Brands

Lympha works with Lotus Coffee Products, using Lotus’s published recipes, and with both Apax Lab ranges: the three-bottle range and the range with KONFLUX. Recipe numbers are shipped exactly as each brand publishes them.

Privacy

Lympha collects no data. The details are in the privacy policy.

Lympha is independent and not affiliated with Lotus Coffee Products or Apax Lab.
