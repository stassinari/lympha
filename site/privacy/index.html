Lympha

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Lympha collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app doesn’t connect to the internet.

What stays on your phone

Your settings and the brand, recipe and volume you last used are saved on your phone, so the app opens where you left off. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

Apple and Google

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your phone’s settings, Apple or Google may pass on anonymous, aggregated figures such as installs and crash counts. These come from the phone’s system, not from Lympha, and are covered by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Lympha is made by Saverio Tassinari. Questions about privacy go to GitHub issues.
