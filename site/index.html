Lympha

Water for coffee

Lympha works out how much of each mineral concentrate to add for the water you’re making, in drops or grams. Pick a brand and a recipe, enter the volume, and the doses are there.

Recipes are written for a litre, so at other volumes the numbers rarely come out whole. Lympha shows what you’ll actually be adding and how far it is from the recipe, and suggests a nearby volume that works out evenly.

Works with Lotus Coffee Products and Apax Lab. No accounts, ads or tracking, and everything works offline.
